# Vidra identity system

The v2 identity uses the V/play mark, outlined Vidra wordmark, warm Paper, and Navy. [Brand guidelines](../guidelines/brand-guidelines.md) govern marketing; [Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/3UVHlVHh4MXHoBUdufD2n7) is the editable visual reference.

Marketing uses a fixed Paper ground with black logo and Navy type. Assets do not respond to OS appearance. Operators may retain their own identity and use the credit badge. Product interface tokens remain in `design-system/tokens.css`; updating marketing assets does not migrate product UI colors.
