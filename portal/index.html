Vidra brand · version 1.1

Run your own video platform.

Vidra is a federated video platform you install yourself, the way you would install WordPress. One low-cost server serves thousands or millions of viewers because egress is distributed rather than paid for.

These are the rules for how Vidra looks and speaks — everywhere outside the product interface. Inside the product, the design system governs.

01

Foundation

What Vidra is, how it sounds, and what it promises. Everything after this page is a rule; this section is the reason the rules exist.

Mission

Video hosting anyone can own

One small server holds the origin. Distribution is offloaded to the people watching, to a CDN, or to IPFS — so the cost of an audience does not scale with the size of it.

Personality

Plain, precise, confident

Vidra says the number, not the adjective. It uses the real term — ActivityPub, egress, CID, instance — and writes for the person reading the docs, not the person skimming a homepage.

Promise

No vendor between you and your viewers

AGPL v3, federation on by default, one compose file. You hold the domain, the database and the media, and your egress bill stays flat as the audience grows.

Where the product is today

Vidra is at v0.5.0, and one command installs it

Say the version when it matters and keep it current: a version number is a fact, "early access" is an adjective. The install line is the shortest true description of the product, so it belongs on the surfaces that describe it.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yegamble/vidra/main/install.sh | sh

Three proof points

01

Low egress at scale

Peers and IPFS carry distribution, so bandwidth cost stops tracking viewer count.

02

Federated by default

ActivityPub and ATProto per channel, not bolted on afterwards.

03

Yours to run

AGPL v3, one compose file, no vendor between you and your viewers.

02

The mark

One otter, four sizes, no variations. The mark is the only drawn asset the brand owns. It is supplied as vector and is never redrawn, recoloured, outlined, rotated or set inside another shape.

Clear space

Half the mark's height on every side. Nothing enters the dashed frame — not type, not a rule, not the edge of the page.

Minimum sizes

Below 16px the silhouette stops reading. Set the mark at these sizes rather than between them.

Approved backgrounds

Paper #F5F5F7 and Ink #0C2136 are preferred. Ice #CBF2F4 is permitted. On photography or a video still, the mark sits behind a 45% Ink scrim — never directly on the image.

03

Lockups

The logotype is drawn: "Vidra" as fixed vector outlines at the brand's −0.045em tracking, kerned by hand. It renders identically on every platform, in print, and in forced-colour contexts.

Use the drawn form for logo placements

Lockups, banners, headers, decks and print take the drawn logotype. Inline and UI text still sets "Vidra" live in the system face at 700 and −0.045em, so the two forms read as one voice. The horizontal lockup's gap equals a third of the mark's width; it is baked into the file, so never rebuild the lockup by hand.

The boundary

Cyan never becomes an interactive colour inside the app, and indigo never appears on a brand surface. The handoff happens at the sign-in screen: seeing indigo tells you that you are in the product; seeing cyan tells you that you are reading about it.

05

Typography

One face, tighter as it gets bigger. The brand uses the same system stack as the product, so a marketing page and the app are set in the same face. Nothing is licensed, nothing downloads.

The stack

-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif

The native UI face on every platform, with a Helvetica Neue fallback. This page — like the logotype, like the product — asks for nothing over the network.

Two weights, sentence case

Regular for reading, bold for headings. Semibold exists for 13–15px labels. Light and black are not part of the brand. Headlines, buttons, nav and section labels are sentence case, always; all caps is reserved for 11px micro-labels.

06

Voice

Say the number, not the adjective. Vidra's audience installs software on servers — they can tell when a claim is measurable and when it is decoration. Write for the person reading the docs, not the person skimming the homepage.

Plain

The shortest true sentence wins.

Factual

Every claim has a number or a mechanism.

Confident

No hedging, no "simply", no apologising for complexity.

Precise

Use the real term: ActivityPub, egress, CID, instance.

Write this, not this

Write this Not this
Serve a million viewers from one small server.Unleash the power of decentralised video at scale.
Egress stays flat because peers carry the bytes.Revolutionary bandwidth savings you have to see to believe.

You, not we

The reader runs the instance: "you install", "your instance", "your viewers". Vidra is the thing, not the protagonist. A sentence that starts with "we" is usually a sentence about the project rather than about the reader's server.

No emoji

Not in the product, where CI enforces it, and not in the docs, the release notes, the commit messages or on social. Icons are drawn glyphs from the product's set — the check and cross on this page are drawn vectors, not characters.

07

Messaging

One line does the work. It appears in the banner, site heroes, social bios and meta descriptions — always with the full stop.

The line · chosen 2026-08

Run your own video platform.

The shortest true sentence — the voice's own first principle — leading on ownership, which is the reason people self-host at all.

Two supporting lines

One small server. A million viewers.

The hardest-to-match technical claim, and concrete enough to be checked.

Video hosting you own, at egress you can afford.

Names the cost problem out loud; the most specific about who it is for.

They support the line in longer formats — section heads, decks, docs intros. They never replace it.

The paragraph

Vidra is a federated video platform you install yourself, the way you would install WordPress. One low-cost server serves thousands or millions of viewers because egress is distributed rather than paid for. It federates over ActivityPub and ATProto, stores media on IPFS if you want it to, and it is free software under AGPL v3.

Three proof points

Low egress at scale

Peers and IPFS carry distribution.

Federated by default

ActivityPub and ATProto per channel, not bolted on.

Yours to run

AGPL v3, one compose file, no vendor between you and your viewers.

What Vidra is not

Not a hosted service

There is no vidra.com to sign up for. You install it.

Not a creator monetisation platform

No payouts, no ad network, no revenue share to describe.

Not a PeerTube fork

A clean-room implementation. Say so plainly when asked.

Imagery & layout

Show the product, or show nothing. No photography or illustration library exists; until one does, the only sanctioned imagery is the interface itself — real screens, real thumbnails, real terminal output.

Screens

A real screen sits here: 16px radius, no drop shadow, cropped to the region that proves the point.

Screens sit on Ink or Paper. Never show full browser chrome. Video stills come from real videos on real instances, credited, cool-toned and un-graded — no filters, no vignette, no cyan wash. Stock photography of people at laptops is not part of this brand.

Every gap, pad and offset on a brand surface is a multiple of 4. Sections take 96px desktop and 48px mobile; cards take 24px inside and 16px between.

Radius · concentric

Radii nest: an inner corner is never larger than the corner containing it. Screens and media use 16, the card radius, on every surface.

Accessibility

Contrast

Body text clears WCAG 2.2 AA at 4.5:1, and 3:1 for large type and UI edges. The product enforces this in CI — axe serious and critical findings block the build — and brand surfaces follow the same bar. Colour never carries meaning on its own: a state always has a glyph or a word next to it.

Focus

Every interactive element shows a visible 2px focus ring with a 2px offset: Action Cyan on Paper, Ice on Ink. Tab through this page to see it. Removing the ring, or replacing it with a colour change alone, is a defect.

Motion

Transitions run 150ms for fills and 200ms for lifts, and never exceed 300ms. Everything honours prefers-reduced-motion, including this page's smooth scrolling and its hover lifts. One exception exists: an infinite ambient animation may ship with a written argument for why a one-shot cannot say the same thing — two are on record — and it stops dead under reduced motion.

Misuse

The six ways the brand gets broken in practice. The examples on the right are wrong on purpose — the mark in them is the real file with a CSS filter over it, never a modified asset.

Also

The mark has six fixed colours and no single-colour version exists yet, so there is no "just make it white" fallback — ask instead. Cyan never becomes a button colour inside the app, and the tri-protocol ribbon belongs to the product: it is the only gradient the identity owns.
